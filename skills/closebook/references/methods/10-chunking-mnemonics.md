# 10 Chunking and memory aids (分块与记忆辅助)

**Principle.** Arbitrary items are easier to hold when grouped into a few meaningful chunks or tied to a vivid cue, and cues the learner builds stick better than cues handed over.

## Fits / doesn't fit
- ✓ `factual` only (lists, codes, names, dates, irregular forms).
- ○ the factual part of `language_production` (vocabulary lists, irregular stems).
- ✗ `conceptual`, `procedural`, `judgment`, `contested`: mnemonics memorise surface form, these need understanding.

## Techniques
| Technique | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Chunking | 5+ items | Group 9 Cache-Control directives into "who may store" / "how long" / "must revalidate" |
| Acronym / first letters | Ordered list | Learner invents a word from first letters |
| Story linking | Unordered items that must all be recalled | A short scene connecting the items |
| Memory palace | Long ordered lists, learner is willing | Place items along a familiar route |
| Keyword method | Vocabulary | Sound-alike word in the learner's language + an image |

## Procedure
1. Retrieve first: test the items once before any aid, so you know which ones are actually hard.
2. Offer one technique for the hard items, with the reason ("these three are arbitrary, so a hook helps").
3. **The learner builds the aid.** You may give the format and one example of a *different* item; don't build their mnemonic for them. Self-generated cues are more memorable, and building it is itself processing.
4. If they're stuck after one try, offer two partial ideas to choose from and let them finish it.
5. Retest the items without the aid visible. Record the aid in `mnemonics[]` so reviews can remind them of their own hook (as a level-1 hint).

## Example (zh)
> **Coach:** 这三个状态码你刚才混了：301、302、304。它们之间没有逻辑关系可推，所以用记忆钩子。给你一个格式例子（用别的东西）：用"404 = 找不着"的谐音法。现在你来给 301/302/304 各造一个钩子。
> **Learner:** 301"永久搬家"——"三零一，一去不回"；302"临时"——"二是暂时"；304"没改"——"四 = 是，还是原来的"。
> **Coach:** 都是你自己的，好。现在不看上面，说出三个码各自的意思。

## Scoring
The aid itself isn't scored. The retest after it is scored as retrieval.

## Progress fields
`mnemonics[]` (strings, the learner's own words); attempts with `method: "chunking_mnemonics"`.
